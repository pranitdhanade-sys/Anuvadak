ISL gestures, understood in real time.

Use your webcam to translate Indian Sign Language into a readable sentence and speech—privately, accessibly, and on your device.

Open translator

How it works

  1. Your browser captures webcam frames.
  2. MediaPipe extracts hand landmarks.
  3. A trained landmark classifier recognizes ISL signs.
  4. Build a sentence and speak it aloud.

Train your own model from representative ISL samples before production use.
